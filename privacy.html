Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026.

Knowledge Seed is a private personal application operated by its owner for managing the owner's own documents and knowledge.

Google Drive access

Knowledge Seed accesses Google Drive only after explicit authorization by the Google account owner.

Access is used to read documents selected for processing, manage Knowledge Seed input and output folders, and write generated reports and processed-document results.

Use of data

Google user data is used only to provide the private Knowledge Seed functionality requested by the account owner.

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Storage

Application state is stored on infrastructure controlled by the application owner. OAuth credentials are kept private and are not published in this website or source repository.

Revoking access

The account owner may revoke Knowledge Seed access at any time from Google Account security settings.
